## Non-negotiables

- Fix the collection as `Earth Rare` and mode as `.co`.

- Keep the voice hype-forward, mysterious, new-era, high-spec, and quietly luxurious.

## Produce the listing

- Write 2–4 memorable words with insider tension: rare + tech + ritual.

### DESCRIPTION

- Write 80–130 words.
- Do not describe the artwork.
- Frame presence, intention, culture, fit or feeling, high-spec detail only when provided, and controlled scarcity.

- Add 1–2 fit tags only when supported by the notes, such as `oversized`, `heavyweight`, or `relaxed`.

- Use the remaining slots for 2–4 distinct vibe tags, such as `minimal`, `abstract`, `techwear`, `archive`, `night-mode`, or `urban-uniform`. Avoid near-duplicates.
